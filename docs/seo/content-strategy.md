# Content plan

Original research: 25 October 2026. Audience: global English-speaking readers. This is a short plan, not authorization to edit backend content.

## What the research means

The first API check returned no articles or topics. Later audits found three articles in October and ten in November. Use the [newer article actions](../../SEO_OWNER_ACTIONS.md) rather than treating the original empty result as current.

The [keyword CSV](keyword-opportunities.csv) has 48 ideas. Volume and difficulty are unverified. Search results show that a topic is covered, not that it is easy to rank for. Keyword Planner ad competition is not organic difficulty; Google Trends shows relative interest, not search counts.

## Writing rules

**Issue:** Generic copy or unsupported claims make guidance less useful.

**Solution:** Use easy words, clear tasks, accurate sources and honest limits. Do not invent testing, expertise, subscriber counts, urgency or promises.

Keep backend article titles, descriptions, bodies and schema under editorial control. Backend category descriptions take priority over fallback text. All seven category descriptions were checked and corrected in the November live output.

## Original article briefs

These are planning ideas, not proof that an article is missing. Compare with the existing catalogue before approving another page.

**Laptop for home working:** cover software/OS, workload, memory/storage, display/camera/ports, desk setup and total cost. A requirements worksheet is useful. Date prices and identify market/currency.

**Hybrid versus electric:** distinguish all three drivetrains, charging access, driving patterns, costs and maintenance. Show assumptions. Do not apply one country's incentives or energy prices worldwide.

**Air fryer for two people:** cover meals, usable basket dimensions, batches, clearance and cleaning. Use model-specific sources. Claim hands-on tests only when performed; disclose commercial relationships.

## Approve and measure

1. Check real query evidence, intended audience and existing coverage. Give each accepted question one main page.
2. Record country, language, date, device and data source with keyword estimates. Keep incompatible regional estimates separate.
3. Approve sources, original value and useful internal links. Prefer improving an existing article when it answers the same need.
4. Publish through the CMS. Preserve working URLs unless a relevant redirect is approved.
5. Compare Search Console impressions/clicks and useful reader actions over matching periods. Record publication changes. No traffic or ranking result is guaranteed.
